## ✨ Features

- 🔐 **Secure Google Sign-In** - Your data is protected
- 👥 **Customer Management** - Add customers with names & phone numbers  
- 💸 **Quick Transactions** - Record "I Gave" and "I Got" in seconds
- 📊 **Auto Balance** - See who owes you and who you owe
- 🌙 **Dark/Light Theme** - Beautiful Material 3 design
- 📱 **Works Offline** - Full functionality without internet
- ☁️ **Cloud Sync** - Data syncs across your devices automatically

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Download** the APK using the button above
2. **Install** on your Android phone (enable "Install from Unknown Sources")
3. **Sign in** with your Google account
4. **Add customers** and start tracking money!
