## 📖 What is My Khata?

**My Khata** is a simple, powerful, and secure **digital ledger app** designed to help you track money you give and receive. Whether you are a small business owner managing customer credit (udhar khata), a freelancer tracking client payments, or just someone keeping track of personal loans with friends, My Khata is the ultimate **money tracking app**. 

Built with Flutter and Firebase, this **open-source ledger app** guarantees your financial data is safe, private, and always available—even without an internet connection!

## ✨ Why Choose My Khata?

### 🚀 1. True Offline Sync
Unlike other apps that freeze without internet, My Khata is built with an **offline-first architecture**. You can add customers and record transactions instantly with zero loading time. The app will automatically sync to the cloud once you reconnect!

### 🔐 2. Bank-Grade Security
Your data is tied strictly to your Google account using **Firebase Authentication**. There are no guest accounts, meaning your financial records are completely private and secure.

### ⚡ 3. Lightning Fast Interface
The app features a beautiful, clean **Material 3 Design**. Recording an "I Gave" or "I Got" transaction takes less than 3 seconds. The dashboard instantly calculates your net balance, receivables, and payables.

### 🎨 4. Beautiful Dark & Light Themes
Easily switch between an elegant dark mode and a clean light mode, or let it follow your system settings to reduce eye strain.

## 🚀 How to Install & Use

1. **Download:** Click the **[Download APK](https://github.com/0xrohitsen/MyKhata/releases/download/v1.0.1/MyKhata_v1.0.1.apk)** button above.
2. **Install:** Open the file on your Android device. (You may need to enable *Install Unknown Apps* in settings).
3. **Sign In:** Launch the app and sign in securely with your Google account.
4. **Start Tracking:** Click the `+` button to add a customer, then tap "I Gave" or "I Got" to record money!
